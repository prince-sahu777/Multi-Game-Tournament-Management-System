# Project Overview

## Title
Multi-Game Tournament Management System in C++

## Aim
To develop a console-based C++ application that manages players, teams, tournaments, match scores, rankings, and winners for BGMI, Free Fire, Valorant, and Counter-Strike 2.

## Objectives
- Maintain player and team records.
- Create game-specific tournaments.
- Prevent teams from registering for a tournament of a different game.
- Record tournament-specific scores.
- Sort teams by points and identify the winner.
- Save records in text files.

## Scope
This is an educational mini project. It manages tournament information entered by the user; it does not connect to the games' official servers or verify in-game results.

## Modules
1. Player management
2. Team management
3. Tournament management
4. Team registration
5. Match scoring
6. Rankings and winner declaration
7. Text-file saving

## Limitations
- Records can be saved, but automatic loading at startup is not implemented.
- Team rosters and individual player statistics are not implemented.
- Scores are manually entered and are not verified against official game data.
